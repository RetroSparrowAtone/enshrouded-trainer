Short answers to the most common questions about the Enshrouded Trainer.

Does the trainer work in co-op?+
Yes. The trainer attaches to your local game process, so it works in single-player and in co-op sessions you host. Only the host's client is affected by the toggles.
Will it get me banned?+
Enshrouded is primarily a co-op experience and the trainer is designed for offline and private sessions. Use it only with people who agree to it, and avoid public servers. You are responsible for how you use it.
Does it modify game files?+
No. The trainer is a native DLL injected at runtime. It does not patch or replace game files, so you can remove it at any time by simply closing the game.
Which Windows versions are supported?+
Windows 10 and Windows 11, 64-bit. Run the injector as administrator and make sure your antivirus has the folder excluded, since DLL injection is often flagged heuristically.
